Help & FAQ

Help & FAQ

Quick answers about Hsepid.np.

What is Hsepid.np?

A Nepal-based studio building web apps, AI tools and interactive experiences, founded by Dipesh Kharel. It also runs two optical stores in Kathmandu.

Where are the optical stores?

Shreekunja Optical and Radhakrishna Optical are both at Jadibuti, Narephat, near BP Eye Hospital. Use “View our stores” to call or WhatsApp either one.

How do I contact you?

Email hsepidnp@gmail.com, use the form below, or call or WhatsApp a store from the stores dialog.

How do I change language, theme or text size?

Open Settings (the globe button, or Settings in the mobile bar). Your choices are remembered in this browser.

How do I find a project?

Use the search button at the top (or press /) to search everything, or the search box in Hsepid Tech to filter projects. Star a project to save it as a favorite.

What does this site store about me?

Only your settings, favorites and recently viewed projects, in this browser. See the Privacy notice in the footer. You can clear them any time.

A page or link isn't working. What now?

Try reloading. Projects are hosted on other sites, so they may be temporarily unavailable. If it keeps failing, email us the project name.
